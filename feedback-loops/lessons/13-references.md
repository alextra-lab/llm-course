The research this course draws on. Follow the sources — the course's opinions should belong to its telemetry and the literature, not its author.

This course is built mostly from a real, shipped harness, so it cites few formal papers. The reflective-loop unit ([06-reflection.md](06-reflection.md)) is where the named literature appears.

## Papers

- **Reflexion** — Shinn et al. (2023). An agent critiques its own trajectory in words and uses that as feedback. Supports the reflective loop in Unit 06 ([06-reflection.md](06-reflection.md)).
- **Self-Refine** — Madaan et al. (2023). Iterative self-feedback. Supports the reflective loop in Unit 06 ([06-reflection.md](06-reflection.md)).
- **Building Effective Agents** — Anthropic. Names the same shape as the **evaluator-optimizer** loop, cited in Unit 06 ([06-reflection.md](06-reflection.md)).

> Note: concepts like OODA, open/closed loops, control theory, and OpenTelemetry appear throughout the course but are not academic citations. For the operational side, see the repo's Observability Standard rather than a paper.
