The research this course draws on. These are the works actually cited in the units — follow the
sources; the course's opinions should belong to the literature, not its author. arXiv links are
given where the citing unit provides an ID; where it does not, the venue is listed as written.

## Papers

- **CoALA** — Sumers et al. (*TMLR* 2024). [arXiv:2309.02427](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02427). The memory taxonomy and "memory as a core architectural component" frame — [Unit 1](01-a-taxonomy-of-memory.md).
- **Reflexion** — Shinn et al. (*NeurIPS* 2023). [arXiv:2303.11366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366). Verbal self-feedback stored and reused as memory — [Unit 1](01-a-taxonomy-of-memory.md).
- **MemGPT** — Packer et al. (2023). [arXiv:2310.08560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560). A layered store, OS-style virtual memory for agents — [Unit 2](02-the-naive-baseline.md).
- **GraphRAG** — Edge et al. (Microsoft, 2024). [arXiv:2404.16130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130). Graph-based retrieval-augmented generation — [Unit 4](04-why-a-graph.md).
- **HippoRAG 2** — Gutiérrez et al. (*ICML* 2025). [arXiv:2502.14802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14802). Graph retrieval gains over the vector baseline — [Unit 4](04-why-a-graph.md).
- **GraphRAG-Bench** — Xiang et al. (*ICLR* 2026). [arXiv:2506.05690](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05690). The cost a graph adds on simple fact lookups — [Unit 4](04-why-a-graph.md).
- **Zep / Graphiti** — Rasmussen et al. (2025). [arXiv:2501.13956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13956). A bi-temporal graph memory system — Units [5](05-modeling-memory-as-a-graph.md), [6](06-ingestion-extracting-structure.md), [11](11-the-opinionated-default.md).
- **Generative Agents** — Park et al. (*UIST* 2023). [arXiv:2304.03442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442). Retrieval as recency + importance + relevance — [Unit 7](07-retrieval-and-context-assembly.md).
- **MemoryBank** — Zhong et al. (*AAAI* 2024). Ebbinghaus-curve forgetting applied to an agent — [Unit 8](08-curation-and-lifecycle.md).
- **A-MEM** — Xu et al. (*NeurIPS* 2025). Curation and lifecycle of an agent memory store — [Unit 8](08-curation-and-lifecycle.md).
- **LoCoMo** — Maharana et al. (*ACL* 2024). A very-long-conversation benchmark — [Unit 9](09-measure-before-you-optimize.md).
- **LongMemEval** — Wu et al. (*ICLR* 2025). A benchmark of distinct memory abilities — [Unit 9](09-measure-before-you-optimize.md).
- **Mem0** — Chhikara et al. (2025). [arXiv:2504.19413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19413). Extract, consolidate, and retrieve salient memories — [Unit 11](11-the-opinionated-default.md).
