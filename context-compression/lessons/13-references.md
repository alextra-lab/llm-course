The research this course draws on. These are the works actually cited in the units — follow the
sources; the course's opinions should belong to the literature, not its author. arXiv links are
given where the citing unit provides an ID; where it does not, the venue is listed as written.

## Papers

- **Lost in the Middle** — Liu et al. (*TACL* 2024). [arXiv:2307.03172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172). Position matters: facts mid-context are recalled worst — Units [0](00-the-context-problem.md), [5](05-head-middle-tail.md).
- **RULER** — Hsieh et al. (*COLM* 2024). [arXiv:2404.06654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654). Usable context is well below the advertised window — [Unit 0](00-the-context-problem.md).
- **Levy et al.** (*ACL* 2024). [arXiv:2402.14848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14848). Added length alone degrades reasoning, holding the task fixed — [Unit 0](00-the-context-problem.md).
- **ReadAgent** — Lee et al. (*ICML* 2024). [arXiv:2402.09727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09727). *Gist memory*: keep short summaries, page in detail on demand — [Unit 8](08-offloading-and-paging.md).
- **MemGPT** — Packer et al. (preprint). [arXiv:2310.08560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560). *Virtual context*: page content between an in-window tier and external store — [Unit 8](08-offloading-and-paging.md).
- **LLMLingua** — Jiang et al. (*EMNLP* 2023). [arXiv:2310.05736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05736). Prompt-level (token) compression — [Unit 10](10-prompt-level-compression.md).
- **LLMLingua-2** — Jiang et al. (*Findings of ACL* 2024). [arXiv:2403.12968](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12968). More realistic 2–5× compression on general tasks — [Unit 10](10-prompt-level-compression.md).
- **Prompt-compression survey** — Li et al. (*NAACL* 2025). [arXiv:2410.12388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12388). Frames hard- vs soft-prompt compression — [Unit 10](10-prompt-level-compression.md).
- **500xCompressor** — Li et al. [arXiv:2408.03094](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03094). A cautionary extreme-compression example — [Unit 10](10-prompt-level-compression.md).
